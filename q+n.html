Nicknames & Quotes

Daniel Du's Most Common Nicknames:

  1. Dan Du
    • Dan Du is a nickname that came from the sheer Middle School humor of his classmates that thought it was funny how simple you could make his name.
  2. Danny
    • This nickname is simply a basic spinoff of the name "Daniel" that even teachers call him sometimes.
  3. Duniel Da
    • This nickname comes from freshman year Health class when the class wrote letters to imaginary people and someone wrote to "Duniel Da," which is Daniel Du but if you switch the vowels of his first and last name.
  4. Dan Dog
    • This is the name Daniel uses over many of his social media platforms, found on the home page. He's not sure why, but he has continued to use the name, even for his gaming accounts.
  5. The Pool King
    • This is what Daniel nicknamed himself, as he believes that he's the best pool player in the entire school and proceeds to call himself this nickname after every time he wins.

  3. "Stop laughing boy"

    • This is the most classic quote that Daniel has said for years. Daniel has a very high self-esteem, and makes it well-known when he says things like this.

    • Originating from Halloween of 2026, Daniel was trying to say something was "ridiculous," but mispoke and called it "remarkable."
